🩺 PU-Track: 욕창 예방을 위한 스마트 체위 변경 주기 예측 시스템

PU-Trackis a smart healthcare system designed to prevent pressure ulcers by predicting patient posture change intervals using IoT-based temperature sensing and machine learning models.

This project was initiated to enhance both caregiving efficiency and patient safety in clinical environments.
